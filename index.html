Answers to common shipping questions

📦 How to track my shipment?

Use our tracking system on the Track page with your unique tracking number.

💰 Who pays customs duties - shipper or receiver?

It depends on your agreement. Receiver pays upon delivery agent contact. We'll clarify this before shipping.

🚚 Do you offer doorstep delivery?

Yes! We provide door-to-door delivery service in all 30+ countries we serve.

✈️ How long does airport clearance take?

Standard clearance takes 24-48 hours. Express clearance is available in 4-6 hours for an additional fee.

📞 What if my package is delayed?

Contact our 24/7 support team immediately. We'll investigate and provide compensation if eligible.

🌍 Which countries do you ship to?

We ship to over 30 countries across North America, Europe, Asia, Africa, and the Middle East.
